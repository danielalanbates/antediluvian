## 5. Skeptical verdict (from HANDOFF_2026-11-24)

Would millions play this today? **No.** The engineering is strong: 1,000 players on screen at about 50 fps and 22.6 KB/s per client, on loopback. What the player sees and does does not compete yet with free MMOs that already have millions of players.

Top gaps:
1. Art: far crowds are block figures, and characters are simple.
2. Combat feel: animation weight, hit reactions and sound are unproven.
3. Content depth: generated board quests dominate, and the story is thin after the first hour.
4. Endgame: one battleground, never played over a socket; no rated matchmaking; raids unproven.
5. Real-network play has never been tested: no latency or loss testing, and no real shard under load. There are >50 ms ticks at 1,000 connections that are not root-caused.
6. The web build compiles but has never run in a browser on this branch.
7. Social systems have never had more than one human in them.
8. UI polish: overlapping name plates, clipped labels, HUD overlap.
9. Abuse tooling: honor farming is reduced, not impossible.
10. No audience, store page or retention data, and the theme is niche.
