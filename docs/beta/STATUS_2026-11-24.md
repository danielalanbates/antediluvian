## 5. Skeptical verdict (from HANDOFF_2026-11-24)

Would millions play this today? **No.** The engineering is strong: 1,000 players on screen at about 50 fps and 22.6 KB/s per client, on loopback. What the player sees and does does not compete yet with free MMOs that already have millions of players.

Top gaps:
1. Art: far crowds are block figures, and characters are simple.
2. Combat feel: animation weight, hit reactions and sound are unproven.
3. Content depth: generated board quests dominate, and the story is thin after the first hour.
4. Endgame: one battleground, never played over a socket; no rated matchmaking; raids unproven.
5. Real-network play has never been tested: no latency or loss testing, and no real shard under load. There are >50 ms ticks at 1,000 connections that are not root-caused.
6. The web build compiles but has never run in a browser on this branch.
7. Social systems have never had more than one human in them.
8. UI polish: overlapping name plates, clipped labels, HUD overlap.
9. Abuse tooling: honor farming is reduced, not impossible.
10. No audience, store page or retention data, and the theme is niche.

## 8. Open beta: camps refill, spawn is safe (later 2026-11-24)

Owner decisions: no Apple Developer Program, so Sign in with Apple is dropped for good (the section 4 item is void); no password gate, the game goes out as an open beta at `/play/`. This section fixes the two server/sim defects that block that. Nothing was deployed.

**Root cause 1: the quest-bot stall (cainites 6/8, then 0/8).** When a mob died, its replacement appeared at once at a random point anywhere in the 7,200-unit zone (`rng.point(WORLD_BOUNDS * 0.9)`, only kept out of the inn ring). So a camp, once cleared, was gone for good, and its mobs were scattered across the map. With the server seed, Eden's cainites are a camp of 6 at 730-845 units from the inn, and the other 2 are about 2,770 units away. Uptime itself did nothing. What mattered was that someone had already killed that camp. On the pre-fix code, a sim replay of the bot's hunt (only cainites within 1,000 units of the inn, the bot's sweep area) went 8/8 on run 1, because random replacements happened to land nearby, and **0/8 after 240 s** on run 2. That is the dev shard's run 2. Taming had the same leak: a tamed beast was removed and never replaced.

**Fix 1** (`crates/sim/src/world.rs`): a kill (or a harvest, or a tame) now queues a replacement on `Zone::respawns`. The replacement comes back **25-40 s later at the dead one's own home spot**, with the same species and stats. Camp guards keep their short leash, cave watchers keep their tier, the act boss keeps its boss stats, and mercy-softened mobs stay softened. Every camp and grove now refills, and the zone's population stays exactly what it booted with.

**Root cause 2: an idle level 1 "slain and revived at the shrine".** New characters start at the **Gate of Eden (2950, 0)**, not at the inn. Two caves sit 605 and 709 units from the gate and seed **next-act (Hermon) watchers**: 60 HP, 9 damage, 380 aggro. The Eden starting-area softening does not apply to them, because they are built with the next act's stats. Their 260-unit patrols bring them into aggro range of the gate. Nothing kept mobs away from a spawn point: the 400-unit ring only applied to boot spawns around the inn, and there was no leash at all. In the sim on the pre-fix code, an idle new character was first hit at 17.6-75 s and died at 34-92 s (the timing varies with map iteration order).

**Fix 2: sanctuaries, evade and leash** (`world.rs`; questgen camp guards in `questgen.rs`).
- Sanctuaries: the inn ring (400) in every act, the Gate of Eden (400), and the four outlying graveyards of each act (200).
- No hostile mob has its home in a sanctuary: boot spawns are pushed out radially, and POI camp guards are turned around their POI. Patrol targets never fall inside one.
- A mob never aggroes, chases or hits a player standing in a sanctuary.
- A mob that loses its target (the target died, outran it, or stepped into a sanctuary), or that is dragged more than 900 units from home, **evades**: it walks home immune and resets to full health.
- A mob hit from inside a sanctuary evades instead of taking the blow, so nobody farms from safety.
- Quest camps sit outside the rings (Eden chain camps: 545+ units from the inn), so the quests are unchanged.
- The server now logs `player died name=... act=...`, so a shard's journal shows deaths.

**Tests** (`crates/sim/src/sanctuary_tests.rs`, 8 new):
- Every kill in every act comes back at its home with its stats, not early and not missing.
- The cainite camp serves the chain quest twice in a row on one world.
- Idle new characters survive 10 minutes at the gate, every inn and every graveyard (3 seeds), with no attack and no death.
- No hostile home sits in a sanctuary.
- A mob never follows a player into the inn ring.
- A mob hit from the ring evades unhurt.
- A dragged mob leashes at 900 and resets.
- A tamed beast is replaced.

The questgen `slay` helper now waits for respawns. `cargo test --release`: **sim 154 passed** (146 + 8), **server 27 passed**.
